From motion generation
to behavioral steering.

RoboSteer evaluates three increasingly complex forms of behavioral steerability: Conditional Steering, Constraint Steering, and Compositional Steering.

L112 tasks

Conditional Steering

Generate or complete motion from a conditioning source. The three task families distinguish complete, temporally partial, and spatially partial conditions.

  • Full Conditioning Reproduction · 5
  • Temporal Completion · 4
  • Spatial Completion · 3
L27 tasks

Constraint Steering

Add an explicit behavioral constraint specifying how the action should be performed.

  • Speed · Amplitude · Direction
  • Order · Times · Trajectory
  • Body Restrain
Explore all seven constraints ↓
L31 task

Compositional Steering

Integrate heterogeneous, temporally interleaved instructions into one consistent motion sequence.

  • Interleaved Multi-Source Steering
